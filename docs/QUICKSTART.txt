Preset Wardrobe for ReSkate
===========================

Save every skater outfit you make as a "fit" in your Fit Catalogue, then pick any 10 of them
to load into the game's preset slots.

WORDS USED
  Fit              one saved outfit (clothes, skater and board), with a picture.
  Fit Catalogue    all your saved fits.
  Rotation         a set of up to 10 fits, one per slot, ready to load into the game.
  In-Game Lineup   the 10 presets in skate. right now.
  Slots            the game's own 10 preset spots on the Skaters page.

WHAT'S HERE
  PresetWardrobe.exe        the program. Run it from anywhere.
  PresetWardrobe-plugin.zip    the in-game plugin that takes the pictures.

SETUP (once)
  1. Drag PresetWardrobe-plugin.zip onto the ReSkate Launcher's MODS page.
  2. Start skate. and choose "Turn on" when it asks about PresetWardrobe.
  3. Open PresetWardrobe.exe. Windows may warn about an app from an unknown publisher:
     "More info > Run anyway". Check the file against SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page first.

ADD FITS TO YOUR CATALOGUE
  In skate.: open the Skaters page, SELECT a preset and press F8. Hold still for the countdown.
  The fit and its picture land in your Fit Catalogue, and the game shows "Added ... to your
  Fit Catalogue". Then change that slot to a new outfit and snap again. Repeat any time.
  If Preset Wardrobe can't tell which slot you snapped, it shows the picture and asks
  "Which slot was it?". Click the right one.
  No picture needed? In-Game Lineup > "Add to catalogue" (or "Add all to catalogue").

BUILD A ROTATION
  Fit Catalogue: click up to 10 fits (they're numbered in the order you click), then
  "Make rotation". Or "New rotation" and pick a fit for each slot. In a rotation you can
  Change, move (arrows) or Clear any slot. Empty slots keep whatever is in the game.

LOAD IT INTO THE GAME
  1. CLOSE skate. (top right says "skate. is closed").
  2. Open the rotation and press "Load into game".
  3. Start skate. Your Skaters page now has those fits.
  Before every load the whole profile is backed up (Settings > Open backups) and your
  In-Game Lineup is kept under "Before-load copies". Progress and unlocks are never touched.

SHARING
  "Export" on a rotation, or "Export catalogue", saves a .wardrobe file with pictures.
  Friends use "Import a .wardrobe".

WHERE THINGS ARE KEPT
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\PresetWardrobe        fits, pictures, rotations, backups
  Your game profile:                    %LOCALAPPDATA%\ReSkate\profiles\offline\reskate.sqlite3
